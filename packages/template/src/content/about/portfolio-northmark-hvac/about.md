title: "Engineering, applied to a building."
---

Northmark Heating & Air installs and services heating, cooling and ventilation systems for homes and light commercial buildings around Northmark.

The approach is unglamorous: measure the building, size the equipment to it, commission the installation properly and write the numbers down. Most comfort complaints turn out to be design problems rather than equipment problems, and a measurement is what tells the two apart.

The same technician who diagnoses your system is the one who comes back for it. Continuity is worth more than a bigger dispatch board.
