---
title: "A small practice, deliberately."
---

Halcott Dental Studio is a general and cosmetic dental practice in Halcott, seeing adults and children.

Appointments here are longer than they are in most practices. That is a deliberate trade: fewer patients in a day, and enough time to explain what is happening rather than narrating it over the noise of a handpiece.

A good share of the people who come here have avoided a dentist for a long time. Nobody is lectured about that.

---

**This is a demonstration build.** Halcott Dental Studio is an invented business, created
so that the meridian design family could be shown publicly without linking a
real client's website or a prospect's private mockup. No such company exists,
nobody works there, and nothing above describes anybody's real work.
