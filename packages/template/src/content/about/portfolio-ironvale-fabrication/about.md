title: "A shop, and the people in it."
---

Ironvale Welding & Fabrication is a fabrication shop working with builders, architects and property managers around the Ironvale area.

The work splits about evenly between structural steel that has to carry load and architectural metalwork that has to look right in a finished building. Both come through the same shop and the same set of hands.

We are a small operation on purpose. The person who quotes your job is the person who welds it, which means nothing is lost between the estimate and the shop floor.

---

**This is a demonstration build.** Ironvale Welding & Fabrication is an invented business, created
so that the forge design family could be shown publicly without linking a
real client's website or a prospect's private mockup. No such company exists,
nobody works there, and nothing above describes anybody's real work.
