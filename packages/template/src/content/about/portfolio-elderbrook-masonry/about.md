title: "A trade that has not changed much, done properly."
---

Elderbrook Masonry works on houses and gardens around Elderbrook: repointing and restoration, stone walls and steps, patios and hardscape.

A good deal of the work is repairing somebody else’s. Hard mortar on a soft wall, a patio on a base that was never deep enough, steps that moved the first winter. None of those are exotic failures; they are what happens when the invisible half of the job is skipped.

We take on fewer jobs than we are asked to, and finish the ones we take. That is the whole business model.

---

**This is a demonstration build.** Elderbrook Masonry is an invented business, created
so that the heritage design family could be shown publicly without linking a
real client's website or a prospect's private mockup. No such company exists,
nobody works there, and nothing above describes anybody's real work.
