---
title: Machining in Elmwood Park since 1987
---

KTS Machine Shop has been making and repairing parts in Elmwood Park since 1987.
Stanley Barszcz owns the shop; Chris and Mike work the floor. Between them that
is who you deal with, from the quote to the part in your hand.

Most of the work is quick-turnaround machining for manufacturers: a prototype
that needs proving before a run is committed, a replacement part for a machine
that is no longer supported, or a repair on something that has to be back on the
line this week. If the job is urgent, say so when you call — that is the kind of
work the shop is set up for.

Walk-ins are welcome. Bring the drawing, the sample, or the broken part itself,
and you will get a straight answer about what it takes and how quickly it can be
done.
