title: "The trade behind the drywall."
---

Voltway Electric is an electrical contractor working on homes and small commercial buildings around Voltway.

The work is mostly panel and service upgrades, rewiring in older buildings, lighting, and EV charger installation — which has quietly become a large share of it as more driveways need a dedicated circuit.

Electrical work is judged almost entirely on what happens after we leave, so we label what we install and close out the permits rather than leaving either to the next person.

---

**This is a demonstration build.** Voltway Electric is an invented business, created
so that the apex design family could be shown publicly without linking a
real client's website or a prospect's private mockup. No such company exists,
nobody works there, and nothing above describes anybody's real work.
