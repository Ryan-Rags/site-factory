title: Bring it in and we will take a look
---

K&S Welding & Fabricating is a walk-in welding and metal fabrication shop on
North Street in Bergenfield. Simon owns it and runs it.

Most jobs here are the ones that are too small, too odd, or too urgent for a shop
that wants production runs: a cracked bracket, a gate that no longer closes, a
custom piece that exists only as a sketch or a mock-up. A lot of it gets done
while you wait.

You will be told what it costs before the work starts, and it will be a fair
number. If it is not a job we can do, we will say so rather than take it on and
leave you waiting.

Bring the part in, or send a photo and we will tell you what it takes.
