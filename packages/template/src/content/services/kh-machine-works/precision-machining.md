Bring us a drawing and we will make the part. Bring us the part and we will
measure it and make another one.

Our machines cover most of what a small shop needs: manual and CNC turning,
vertical milling, surface and cylindrical grinding, drilling, tapping,
keyseating and boring. For work that needs a finish or a treatment we do not do
in-house — heat treat, plating, anodising — we manage the outside process and
you still deal with one shop.

Where the job calls for it we supply material certs and inspection dimensions
with the finished part. Where it does not, we will tell you so rather than bill
you for paperwork you do not need.
