title: Replacement Parts
summary: Bring the worn part in and we will make you a new one.
highlights:
  - Made from the original part when no drawing exists
  - For machines that are obsolete or no longer supported
  - Single replacements — no minimum order
  - Measured and checked before it leaves the shop
---

When a machine is old enough that the part is no longer made, the part itself is
the drawing. Bring us the worn one and we will measure it and make a new one.

Where a part has failed rather than simply worn out, tell us how it failed. It
often points at something worth changing in the replacement.
