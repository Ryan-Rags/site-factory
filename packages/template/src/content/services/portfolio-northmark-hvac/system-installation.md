Most replacement systems are sized by reading the label on the old one. That is how a building ends up with equipment that short-cycles, never dehumidifies properly and costs more to run than it needs to.

We calculate the load room by room, size the equipment to that, and check the duct static pressure before we call the job finished. You get the numbers, not just an invoice.
