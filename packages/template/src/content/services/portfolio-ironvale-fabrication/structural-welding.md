Most of the structural work that comes through here arrives as a drawing and a deadline. We read the drawing, tell you what we would change before we cut anything, and then build it.

Work that has to carry load gets treated differently from work that has to look right. Both matter, and they are not the same job — so we say which one we are doing before we quote it.
